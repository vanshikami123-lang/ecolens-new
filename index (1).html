02 — A CLEARER PICTURE

From photo to next step.

EcoLens turns a visual check into a simple, editable estimate — with the assumptions in plain sight.

STEP / 01

Spot the devices

Upload a room photo or use the sample scenario. In this demo, sample detections are simulated, not generated by a vision model.

STEP / 02

Check the assumptions

Confirm the appliance list, choose which devices you believe are running, and edit the estimated wattage for your setup.

STEP / 03

Explore the impact

Compare estimated kWh and cost, change your tariff, and use practical suggestions to decide what to check next.

03 — WHY ECOLENS

Better awareness.
Smarter everyday choices.

EcoLens is a concept for making energy awareness approachable. It connects visible equipment with transparent estimates, helping people ask better questions about energy use without pretending a photo is a power meter.
